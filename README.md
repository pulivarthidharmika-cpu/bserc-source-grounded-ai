## Next Steps for Manual NotebookLM Creation & Submission

1. **Open Google NotebookLM:** Navigate to [notebook.google.com](https://notebook.google.com) and click **Create New**.
2. **Upload the 10 Sources:** Follow the ingestion guide in [`source_list.md`](./source_list.md) (paste URLs, upload PDFs of *ISLP* and *Mathematics for Machine Learning*, and add YouTube links for MIT lectures).
3. **Generate Outputs:** Use the exact prompt templates provided in [`notebooklm_prompts.md`](./notebooklm_prompts.md) to generate your Study Guide, Quiz, Flashcards, Mind Map, and Audio Overview.
4. **Test Every Feature:** Complete the checklist (Source Toggling, Citation Clicking, Off-Topic Stress Test, Mother Tongue translation).
5. **Share & Submit:**
   - Click **Share** in the top-right corner.
   - Set access to *"Anyone with the link can view"*.
   - Copy the link and test it in an Incognito / Private browser window.
   - Submit the link on the BSERC AI portal at [ai.bserc.org/projects](https://ai.bserc.org/projects) under Section 8 (*Demo / Video / Presentation link*).
